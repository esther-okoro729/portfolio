About

People. Growth. Impact.

I am a passionate Human Resources professional with seven years of experience in HR, driven by a genuine interest in people, growth, and organisational development.

My journey in HR has taken me through recruitment, employee relations, performance management, HR operations, learning and development, talent management, and leadership development. Today, I serve as an HR Executive & Talent Management/Learning Officer at Murphy Ben International, where I support both the day-to-day people needs of the organisation and the development of its talent and leadership pipeline.

What I enjoy most about HR is seeing people discover their potential and helping create the environment, opportunities, and support they need to succeed. I am particularly passionate about learning and development, talent management, employee engagement, leadership development, and building positive workplace cultures.

"Effective HR is not just about policies and processes—it is about people, trust, growth, and impact."

I am a PHRi-certified HR professional, a lifelong learner, and a strong believer in the power of people to transform organizations.
